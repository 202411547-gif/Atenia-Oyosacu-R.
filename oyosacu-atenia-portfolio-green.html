My project

A project I developed for my App Dev course.

SlotSense

Smart parking system for Olongapo City

SlotSense helps drivers in Olongapo City find, reserve, and navigate to available parking spots in real time. It aims to save drivers time and make parking less stressful.

What drivers can do
  • Find available parking spots in real time
  • Reserve a spot ahead of arrival
  • Navigate to the reserved spot
